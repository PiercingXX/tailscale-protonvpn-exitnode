I have a question about the routing in your project, but there's no way 
to start a discussion or raise an issue. Your bio says you don't want 
emails and that issues should be raised in the relevant repo, so this is 
the only option left to me.

The issue is that all traffic is directed through the VPN, even the 
tailscale control traffic and tunnel itself. This causes enormous amounts 
of latency, and even double tunneling that could cause MTU issues. 
Hopefully you had that in mind, because in this case the MTU budget works 
out, and there's no excess fragmenting going on. However, as it is now, it 
is a horrible idea to run this container on your local network for the 
purpose of offering vpn connectivity to your tailscale clients, because 
every little packet has to traverse the internets, enter the vpn tunnel, 
get processed by the exit node, then back out through the tunnel and on 
to wherever it is headed. The return path is the exact same in reverse. 
If it is a security argument, I get it. But at least it should be an 
option to let the tailscale traffic bypass the tunnel to avoid excessive 
latency, in addition to being rate limited by DERP because a direct 
tailscale connection cannot be established. At least that has been my 
experience so far.

Are you aware of this, and are there plans to mitigate? I appreciate your 
effort on this project, but in my opinion this needs to be handled to make 
it performant and useful for those of us that aren't paranoid about 
potentially revealing the container public address. Unless you're knee 
deep in working out a solution already, I'd be happy to take a stab at it.
